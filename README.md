# Stella's Game

A warm, voice-first early learning PWA for little ones (about 0-5 years).

Tap, listen, drag, paint, and trace — with short spoken prompts so children do not need to read.

## Play

- Live site: https://0mrcrazy0.github.io/stella-game/
- Or open `index.html` from this folder in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.

For the best phone experience:

1. Open the site in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone)
2. Add to Home Screen
3. Play from the icon

## What is inside

- 0-12 months / Sensory Fun: moving shapes, music pads, farm animals, peekaboo, ABC song
- 1-2 years / Colors: tap the color, flashcards, say the color, match colors
- 2-3 years / Shapes: shape homes (drag), biggest shape, find a shape, pop shapes
- 3-4 years / Numbers: count stars, more or less, tap a number, count together
- 4-5 years / Letters: picture match, bubble pop, letter sounds, picture words
- Create: finger paint, letter trace, number trace, shape trace

## Voice

Uses the device text-to-speech voice. Install a male English TTS voice on the phone if you want that sound.

## Farm sounds

## ABC song

Needs internet. If the in-game player is blank, use Open YouTube.

## Privacy

No accounts. No tracking scripts. Photos from Capture a moment stay on the device.
